## Full description (4000 chars max)

🌱 **Your fresh start starts now.**

BudFree is your personal quit-weed companion — part tracker, part game, part supportive buddy. Whether you're quitting for your health, your wallet, your mind, or the people you love, BudFree turns the hardest journey into a rewarding adventure.

**⏱️ LIVE SOBRIETY COUNTER**
Watch every second of progress tick by — days, hours, minutes. Plus a real-time "money saved" counter based on what you used to spend.

**🤖 MEET BUD, YOUR AI BUDDY**
Tell Bud why you're quitting and what triggers you, and get support tailored to YOU. Craving? Stressed? Can't sleep? Slipped? Bud responds with zero judgment and real strategies. Works fully offline, or connect a free Groq API key for full AI conversations.

**🆘 PANIC BUTTON**
When a craving hits hard, hit the button. Guided breathing with a calming animated bubble gets you through the wave — and beating it earns you XP and coins.

**🌊 CRAVING LOG**
Name it to tame it. Log what you're craving, how intense, and what triggered it. Spot your patterns. Slips are data, not defeat.

**📊 BEAUTIFUL STATS**
A 12-week heatmap of your clean days and a craving intensity trend chart — watch yourself get stronger week by week.

**🏅 ACHIEVEMENTS & LEVELS**
Unlock badges from "First Hour" to the legendary 1-year crown. Earn XP, level up from Seed to Ancient Forest, and keep daily check-in streaks alive with freeze cards.

**🛍️ REWARD SHOP**
Spend your hard-earned coins on gorgeous app themes — Vista Sunset, Aurora Nights, Deep Forest. Quitting literally pays off.

**🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN**
No account. No ads. No tracking. Your data never leaves your phone. The optional AI feature uses your own API key — we never see it.

Every day clean is a day your brain heals. Let BudFree prove it to you. 💚
