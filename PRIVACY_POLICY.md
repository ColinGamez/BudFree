# BudFree Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 13, 2026

BudFree ("the app") is a quit-weed companion built with privacy as a core principle.

## The short version
We collect **nothing**. Your data lives on your phone and never leaves it — unless you explicitly enable the optional AI chat feature with your own API key.

## What the app stores (locally, on your device only)
- Your quit date and typical daily spend (for the counters)
- Check-in streaks, XP, coins, achievements
- Craving logs you choose to record (type, intensity, notes)
- Buddy personalization (name, quit reason, trigger, goal)
- App preferences (theme, owned shop items)

All of the above is stored in local on-device storage (AsyncStorage). It is never transmitted to us — we have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking SDKs.

## Optional AI feature (Groq)
BudFree offers an **optional** AI chat upgrade powered by Groq. If you choose to enable it:
- You provide your **own** Groq API key, which is stored only on your device.
- Chat messages and basic app stats (days clean, streak, etc.) are sent directly from your device to Groq's API to generate replies. This traffic is governed by [Groq's privacy policy](https://groq.com/privacy-policy/).
- We (the app developer) never see, store, or have access to your key or your conversations.

If you never add a key, nothing is ever sent anywhere.

## Permissions
- **Notifications**: used only for check-in reminders and milestone alerts you can disable in system settings.

## Data deletion
Since all data is on-device, uninstalling the app (or clearing app data in Android settings) permanently deletes everything.

## Children's privacy
BudFree is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the app's repository and listing.
